Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026

  • We don't collect your data. Arch has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads.
  • Your documents are encrypted on your device with keys only you hold.
  • Backups are optional and go, still encrypted, to your own cloud account (Google Drive, iCloud, Dropbox or OneDrive). We can't read them.
  • We never sell or share your data, because we never have it. To delete everything, see Delete your data.

1. Who we are

Arch is made by NADESOFT ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy, email contact@nadesoft.bd.

2. How Arch works

Arch is a local-first app. Everything you add (photos and PDFs of documents, titles, numbers, dates, notes, family profiles, trips and your Emergency Medical card) is stored on your device, encrypted with AES-256. The encryption keys are created on your device and kept in its secure hardware keystore (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore), unlocked with your biometrics or device passcode. A Recovery Key is shown to you once during setup. We never receive it.

NADESOFT does not run any server for Arch. There is no Arch account and nothing to sign up for.

3. Information we collect

None. NADESOFT does not collect, receive or store any personal data through Arch.

The table below describes data that stays under your control, and where it goes:

Data Where it goes Who can read it
Documents, metadata, profiles, trips, medical card Your device (encrypted). If you turn on backup: your own cloud account (encrypted). Only you, on a device you've unlocked or with your Recovery Key.
Cloud sign-in tokens Your device's secure keystore. Arch on your device, to reach your own cloud folder.
Encrypted backup file (.arch) Wherever you choose to save it. Only someone with your Recovery Key.
Emergency card fields you choose to show on the lock screen (optional, off by default) Your device's widget/notification storage, not encrypted, so they can be shown without unlocking. Anyone holding your phone. Only the fields you select.

4. What we don't do

  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or advertising IDs.
  • No crash-reporting SDKs and no remote logging.
  • No location, contacts or microphone access.
  • No push notification service. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device.
  • No cloud text recognition. Scanning and reading of dates and passport details happens on your device.

5. Device permissions

Permission Why
Camera To scan documents. Scans go straight into the encrypted vault, not your photo library.
Photos / files To import a document you pick, and (if you ask) to delete the original photo.
Face ID / fingerprint To unlock the vault. Biometric data is handled by your device's operating system and never seen by Arch.
Notifications For expiry reminders and the yearly Vault check-up, scheduled on your device.

Every permission is optional and requested only when you first use the related feature.

6. Third-party services you may use with Arch

Arch talks directly from your device to these services. NADESOFT is not in the middle:

  • Cloud backup (optional): Google Drive, Apple iCloud, Dropbox or Microsoft OneDrive. Arch only requests access to its own app folder, not your other files. The provider stores encrypted data in your account under its own privacy policy (Google, Apple, Dropbox, Microsoft). The provider can see the encrypted files' sizes and when they change, but not their contents.
  • Purchases: Arch Pro is sold through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google process your payment. Arch checks your purchase on your device and never receives your payment details.
  • On-device text recognition (Android and iOS): Arch uses Google ML Kit's on-device models to read document text. Images and recognised text stay on your device. ML Kit may send Google anonymous technical information about how the library performs (for example, which device model is in use), under Google's ML Kit terms.
  • Operating system diagnostics: if you have chosen to share diagnostics with Apple or Google, they may share anonymous crash reports with developers. This is controlled in your device settings, not by Arch.

7. Sharing

We don't share or sell personal data. When you share a document from Arch (for example, a watermarked copy for a hotel), you choose the recipient and app. Arch removes location and camera metadata from the exported copy and deletes the temporary file afterwards.

8. Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device and in your cloud for as long as you keep it there. Deleted documents go to "Recently deleted" for 30 days, then they are permanently removed from your device and your backup. You can erase everything at any time. See Delete your data.

9. Your rights

Wherever you live, including under the EU and UK GDPR and California's CCPA/CPRA, you have rights to access, correct, export and delete your personal data. Because NADESOFT holds no personal data about Arch users, you exercise these rights directly in the app: view and edit any document, export an encrypted backup, or erase your vault. If you have a question or complaint, email contact@nadesoft.bd. We reply within 30 days.

10. Security

Documents are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using keys generated on your device. Backups are encrypted before they leave your device. Arch locks automatically when you leave it, hides its content in the app switcher and blocks screenshots of documents on Android. No system is perfect: keep your device passcode secure and store your Recovery Kit somewhere safe. If you lose both your devices and your Recovery Key, nobody (including NADESOFT) can recover your backup.

11. Children

Arch is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone. Parents and guardians may store their children's documents in their own vault using family profiles. That data stays under the parent's control on their device and in their own cloud.

12. Sensitive information

Identity documents, prescriptions and medical details are sensitive. Arch is designed so that this information is encrypted at all times and never reaches NADESOFT. The only exception is the lock-screen Emergency card, which you can turn on and limit to the fields you choose.

13. This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or third-party scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.

14. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app before they take effect.
