Encrypted on your phone. Stored in your cloud. Never on ours.

  • Every document is encrypted on your device before it is saved or backed up.
  • Backups go to your own Google Drive, iCloud, Dropbox or OneDrive, still encrypted.
  • Arch has no servers and no accounts. We can't see your documents. Nobody else can either.

Two-second retrieval

Face ID or fingerprint opens straight to your pinned cards. Works fully offline.

Smart scanning

Auto-cropped scans. Passport details and expiry dates are read on your device, never uploaded.

Expiry reminders

Reminders well before passports, licences and policies expire, including the six-month passport rule.

Safe sharing
