Security & recovery

Can NADESOFT see my documents?

No. Arch has no servers. Your documents are encrypted on your device and only backed up, still encrypted, to your own cloud account.

What is the Recovery Kit and why does it matter?

During setup Arch shows a 12-word Recovery Key and a QR code. It's the only way to restore your backup on a new phone. Print it or write it down and keep it somewhere safe, away from your phone. We can't recover it for you.

I lost my Recovery Key. What can I do?

If you can still open Arch on any device, go to Settings → Recovery Kit and create a new one. The old key stops working. If you have lost every device and the key, the backup can't be decrypted by anyone.

Face ID / fingerprint stopped working in Arch

Use your device passcode instead. If you removed your device passcode, your phone deletes Arch's device key for security. Open Arch and enter your Recovery Key to set it up again. No documents are lost.

Backup & new devices

How do I back up my vault?

Settings → Backup → choose Google Drive, iCloud (iPhone and iPad), Dropbox or OneDrive. Everything is encrypted before upload. You can also use Settings → Export encrypted backup to save a single .arch file anywhere, with no cloud account needed.

How do I move to a new phone?

Install Arch, tap "I already use Arch", connect the same cloud account and scan or type your Recovery Key. Or open your .arch file with Arch and enter the key.

I can see an "Apps/Arch" folder in Dropbox or OneDrive

That's your encrypted backup. Please don't edit, move or delete files in it. If something goes missing, Arch offers to upload everything again from your phone.

Reminders

I'm not getting expiry reminders

Check that notifications are allowed for Arch in your phone's settings, and that the document has an expiry date. Reminders are scheduled on your device, so battery-saver modes that close apps can delay them. Open Arch occasionally to refresh them.

Why remind me about my passport nine months early?

Many countries require your passport to be valid for six months after you arrive, and renewals take time. You can change the reminders for any document.

Sharing

How do I send a copy safely?

Open the document → Share. Choose a purpose such as "Hotel check-in". Arch adds a dated watermark, lets you black out numbers, removes photo metadata, and deletes the temporary copy afterwards.

Family, trips & emergency card

Can I store my children's documents?

Yes. Add a person in the profile switcher and choose them as the owner when saving a document. Their documents stay in your vault.

How does the lock-screen Emergency card work?

It's off by default. If you turn it on, only the fields you choose (for example blood type and allergies) are shown without unlocking your phone, so first responders can read them. Turning it off removes them immediately.

Arch Pro & purchases

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Use the same Apple ID or Google account, then open Settings → Restore purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription or get a refund?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.

What happens to my documents if Pro ends?

They all stay viewable and shareable. Only Pro features (such as backup and unlimited reminders) pause.
