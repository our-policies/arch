Your data

Delete your data

App: Arch · Developer: NADESOFT

Arch has no accounts and NADESOFT stores no data about you. Everything lives on your device and, if you turned on backup, in your own cloud account. You can delete all of it yourself, right away, using the steps below.

1. Delete your cloud backup (if you use one)

  1. Open Arch and unlock it.
  2. Go to Settings → Backup.
  3. Tap Delete cloud backup and confirm with Face ID, fingerprint or your passcode.

This removes every Arch file from your Google Drive, iCloud, Dropbox or OneDrive.

2. Erase the vault on your device

  1. Go to Settings → Security.
  2. Tap Erase vault on this device and confirm.
  3. Repeat on any other device where you use Arch, then delete the app.

This permanently deletes all documents, profiles, trips, the Emergency card and the device's encryption keys.

3. Remove Arch's access to your cloud (optional)

  • Google: Google Account → Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services → Arch → Delete all connections.
  • Dropbox: Settings → Connected apps → Arch → Disconnect.
  • Microsoft: account.microsoft.com → Privacy → Apps and services → Arch → Remove these permissions.
  • iCloud (iPhone/iPad): Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Arch → Delete Data.

If you can't use the app

If you no longer have the app or the device, you can delete your encrypted backup directly in your cloud account: remove the Apps/Arch folder in Dropbox or OneDrive, or use the steps in section 3 for Google and iCloud. Google Drive's hidden app data is deleted when you choose Delete all connections for Arch, or from Google Drive on the web: Settings → Manage apps → Arch → Options → Delete hidden app data.

Because NADESOFT never receives your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. If you need help, email contact@nadesoft.bd with the subject "Arch data deletion" and we will guide you within 14 days.

What is kept

  • Purchase records are kept by Apple or Google under their own policies. NADESOFT doesn't hold them.
  • Encrypted backup files you exported yourself (.arch files) stay wherever you saved them until you delete them.
  • Copies you shared with other people or apps are outside Arch's control.
